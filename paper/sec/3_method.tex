\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Model and metrics.} We use the open\_clip ViT-B/32 (QuickGELU) pretrained on LAION-400M~\cite{cherti2023openclip,radford2021clip}: 12 image blocks of width 768 (MLP $768{\to}3072{\to}768$) and 12 text blocks of width 512. All experiments run on CPU on cached block inputs: for every evaluation image we store the input tokens of every image block, so a change in block $k$ costs blocks $k..11$. Metrics are zero-shot top-1 on stratified 800-image subsets of Imagenette, Oxford-IIIT Pets and CIFAR-10 (seven prompt templates, mean of the three reported as \emph{mean\_acc}) and image-to-text R@1/R@5 on 1\,000 COCO images with category-templated pseudo-captions (73 distinct captions). The clean model scores 0.985 / 0.834 / 0.890 and R@1 0.224.

\paragraph{HTSR $\alpha$.} For every weight matrix we fit the power-law tail of the eigenvalues of $W^\top W$ with WeightWatcher 0.7.7 (\texttt{min\_evals}=50). Attention in-projections are split into $q$, $k$, $v$; embedding tables are reported but never averaged with dense layers, following our companion study on lookup layers.

\paragraph{Replace-activation insert (conversion).} The QuickGELU of one block's MLP is replaced by a population of integrate-and-fire units driven for $T$ steps by the constant pre-activation $I$: $u_t = u_{t-1} + I - \theta\, s_{t-1}$, $s_t = \mathbb{1}[u_t > \theta]$, $u_0 = \theta/2$, output $g \cdot \tfrac{1}{T}\sum_t s_t + b$ with per-channel gain $g$ (initialised to $\theta$) and offset $b$. For $T \to \infty$ this is $\min(\mathrm{ReLU}(I), \theta) + b$, so the conversion error is the GELU-vs-clipped-ReLU shape error plus the $1/T$ quantisation~\cite{rueckauer2017conversion}. The backward pass uses a sigmoid surrogate of slope $k_{\mathrm{sg}}$~\cite{neftci2019surrogate}.

Two details are decisive for CLIP. \emph{Per-channel thresholds:} $\theta_j$ is the 99.9th percentile of channel $j$'s pre-activation on 256 calibration images. CLIP blocks contain a few channels whose pre-activation reaches 15 (image) or 93 (text) while the pooled 99.9th percentile is 0.6--2.3, so a single threshold clips exactly the channels that carry the signal (relative MLP output error 0.98 in text block 1 against 0.09 for plain ReLU). \emph{Bias correction:} only 2--7\,\% of pre-activations are positive, so the MLP output is dominated by the negative lobe of GELU ($x\sigma(1.702x) \in [-0.17, 0)$) summed over 3\,072 channels; a ReLU-like rate code discards it and the relative MLP output error is 0.64--1.27 (zero-shot accuracy drops 21--24 points for a single early block). We fold the calibration mean of $\mathrm{GELU}(x) - \min(\mathrm{ReLU}(x), \theta)$ per channel into the offset $b$, which brings the error to 0.12--0.28 and the accuracy drop to 1--2 points. The correction is a constant per channel and costs nothing at inference.

\paragraph{Spiking adapter (addition).} A bottleneck $W_{\mathrm{up}}\,\mathrm{SpikingAct}(W_{\mathrm{down}}\, x)$ with rank 64 added in parallel to a block's MLP, $W_{\mathrm{up}}$ zero-initialised so that the model is unchanged at insertion; the dense twin uses QuickGELU.

\paragraph{Fine-tuning.} Only the modified block's MLP ($W_{\mathrm{fc1}}, W_{\mathrm{fc2}}$, LayerNorm) or the adapter trains, with Adam, on 2\,000 Imagenette-train images for three epochs, under a distillation objective $1 - \cos(e, e_{\mathrm{clean}})$ to the clean model's embedding of the same image (the standard conversion-repair objective); a text-anchored cross-entropy variant is reported as an ablation. $\alpha$ of the trained matrices, the fraction of silent and saturated units, the mean rate and the five metrics are logged every 24 steps / every epoch.

\paragraph{Cumulative conversion.} Blocks are converted one after another with sequential calibration in six orders: $|\alpha_{\mathrm{mlp}}-2|$ ascending and descending (mean of fc1 and fc2 $\alpha$ of the pretrained block), depth-first, depth-last and two random permutations, at 3, 6, 9 and 12 converted blocks.

\paragraph{Adapters on frozen embeddings.} CLIP-Adapter-style residual adapters on the 512-d image embedding: a dense MLP ($512{\to}256{\to}512$), its spiking twin, a dense L3 lookup memory~\cite{tseng2026l3} keyed by a 64-way $k$-means token of the embedding (8 entries per token), and a spiking L3 memory in which the embedding drives a LIF population whose spikes query the memory each step, either with threshold-selected entries (\emph{spiking memory}) or a softmax over spike scores (\emph{rate query}). All output projections are zero-initialised.
